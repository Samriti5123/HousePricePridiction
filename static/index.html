Key Drivers for this Evaluation

The regression pipeline evaluated total_sqft as the highest-weight feature, coupled with Whitefield's historical premium and Super built-up Area coefficient.

ML Pipeline & Dataset Architecture

Dataset & Cleaning Pipeline

Trained on 13,320 records of Bengaluru real estate properties. Cleaned missing values, converted sqft ranges to averages, consolidated 1,287 rare locations into 241 standardized locality clusters (frequency > 10), and purged extreme 1% outliers.

Regression Engine
Random Forest Regressor (200 trees)
  • R² Score: 0.761
  • Target: Transformed log(Price) with expm1 inverse
  • Preprocessing: OneHotEncoder for 241 locations & 4 area types
Classification Engine
Random Forest Classifier (200 trees)
  • F1 Macro: 0.7697 (Accuracy: 76.84%)
  • Tiers: Low (≤ 55L), Medium (55-93L), High (> 93L)
  • Calculated from training terciles avoiding data leakage

Key Outlier Rules Applied

  • Minimum Room Size: At least 300 sq.ft per BHK
  • Bathroom Sanity: Bathrooms cannot exceed BHK count by more than 2
  • Price Per Sq.Ft: Truncated extreme 1st and 99th percentiles
